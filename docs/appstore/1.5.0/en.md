## Description

```text
A safe game collection made for kids.
No ads, no in-app purchases, no personal data collected. Works without an internet connection.

■ Games
• MAKE10 – tap number cards to make 10
• Whack-a-Mole – tap the moles before they hide
• Maze (Cheese Quest) – collect the cheese and blast mice away with stored-up shockwaves
• Pinball – hit the ball back with the flippers
• Emoji Quiz – animals, world flags and airport codes
• CoinDrop – drop coins and merge them
• Command Janken – rock-paper-scissors with a twist: win, or lose on purpose, just as the command says
• Ten Puzzle – make 10 with four numbers and + − × ÷
• Animal Match – a memory game with animal cards
• Sticker Book & Drawing – place stickers and draw freely with crayons

■ Thank-You Notebook
Three small missions arrive every day, like "Say thank you to the person who made your meal" or "Say good night." Check them off and choose who you told. Look back on all your thank-yous in the calendar and the summary.

■ Your Drawing as Wallpaper
Turn your drawing into the background of the title screen and the games. Move the drawing tools around or fold them away to draw on the whole screen.

■ Energy and Stickers
Playing games fills up your Energy (measured in kcal). Use it to spin the Gacha, or trade it for stickers at the Sticker Corner, whose lineup changes every day. There are 249 stickers to collect, and you can see them all in the Sticker Collection. Energy only grows by playing — there is no way to buy it with money. The Sticker Corner also shares friendly tips about what "kcal" means.

■ Quizzes About the World
Try the flags of every UN member country (199 countries and regions), plus airport codes from Japan, China, Germany, France, the USA, the Netherlands and over 4,000 airports around the world. Look-alike flags appear side by side, so you learn what makes each one different. Categories are sorted from fewest to most questions, so you can start easy and keep growing.

■ For Parents
• A gentle break reminder appears after 30 minutes of continuous play (you can turn it off in Settings)
• Sound effects and vibration can each be turned on or off
• Works on iPhone and iPad
• Available in 15 languages

■ Open Source
We publish our source code so anyone can check that this app shows no ads, has no purchases and never connects to outside services.
```
