# 審査メモ（App Review への説明）— v1.5.0

App Store Connect の「審査メモ」にそのまま貼る。旗のクイズの表記などで指摘があったら、そのときに対応する。

```text
Version 1.5.0 – Release Notes

This update includes the following changes:

1. Energy and stickers
   Playing games now earns "Energy," an in-game point shown in kcal. Energy can be used for the Gacha (a random sticker) or at the Sticker Corner (a daily selection of stickers). Energy is earned only by playing and cannot be bought with real money. Every sticker in the Gacha has the same chance of appearing. A daily login bonus and a one-time Energy gift for updating users are also included.

2. New feature: Thank-You Notebook
   A simple daily checklist with three small missions, such as saying "thank you" to someone. Children choose who they said it to from a fixed list (e.g., "Mom," "Friend"). No text input is required, and all records are stored only on the device.

3. Drawing as wallpaper
   Children can use their own drawing (made in the app's drawing screen) as the background of the app. The image is saved only on the device. The drawing tools can now be moved or folded away.

4. Quiz updates
   The flag quiz now covers all UN member states. New airport code quizzes have been added for several countries and for airports worldwide. All quiz data is included in the app and works offline.

5. Game updates
   New enemy types and stored shockwaves in the Maze game, Energy rewards in Animal Match, and other gameplay adjustments.

6. Break reminder
   After 30 minutes of continuous play, a gentle reminder suggests taking a break. Parents can turn it off in Settings.

7. Other improvements
   Improved layouts on iPad, separate settings for sound effects and vibration, and minor fixes.

There are no changes to the app's data handling or privacy practices. All data, including the drawing used as wallpaper, is stored locally on the device. The app continues to contain no ads, no in-app purchases, and does not collect or transmit any personal information. It does not connect to the internet.

Thank you for your review.
```

## 指摘されたときのメモ
- 旗: アプリ内の呼び名は「はた（Flags）」「せかいの旗（World Flags）」。教育目的で、国連加盟国を基準に選んでいる（台湾・香港・ニューカレドニア・バチカン・パレスチナ・コソボも含む）。
- ガチャ: 全249種が同じ確率。エネルギーは遊んだときだけ増え、お金では買えない。
