## Description（4,000文字まで）

```text
A safe game collection made for kids.
No ads, no in-app purchases, no personal data collected. Works without an internet connection.

■ Games
• MAKE10 – tap number cards to make 10
• Whack-a-Mole – tap the moles before they hide
• Maze (Cheese Quest) – collect the cheese and blast mice away with stored-up shockwaves
• Pinball – hit the ball back with the flippers
• Emoji Quiz – animals, world flags and airport codes
• Coin Drop – drop coins and merge them
• Command Janken – rock-paper-scissors with a twist: win, or lose on purpose, just as the command says
• Ten Puzzle – make 10 with four numbers and + − × ÷
• Animal Match – a memory game with animal, fruit, star sign and month cards
• Sticker Book & Drawing – place stickers and draw freely on 12 pages

■ Animal Match
Choose from six card sets:
• Animals (4×5), Animals (5×5), Fruits
• Star signs, Months (English), Months (kanji) – harder sets that give bonus Energy when you clear them
When you get a star sign sticker, its name and the shape it comes from are shown too.

■ Thank-You Notebook
Three small missions arrive every day, like "Say thank you to the person who made your meal" or "Say good night." Check them off and choose who you told. Look back on all your thank-yous in the calendar and the summary.

■ Your Drawing as Wallpaper
Turn your drawing into the background of the title screen and the games. Move the drawing tools around or fold them away to draw on the whole screen.

■ Energy and Stickers
Playing games fills up your Energy (measured in kcal). Use it to spin the Gacha, or trade it for stickers at the Sticker Corner, whose lineup changes every day. There are 273 stickers to collect, and you can see them all in the Sticker Collection. Stickers you don't use can be turned back into Energy. Finish each game for the first time each day to get bonus Energy. Energy only grows by playing — there is no way to buy it with money.

■ Quizzes About the World
Try the flags of every UN member country (199 countries and regions), plus airport codes from Japan, China, Germany, France, the USA, the Netherlands and over 4,000 airports around the world. Look-alike flags appear side by side, so you learn what makes each one different.

■ For Parents
• Progress can be saved to your own iCloud, so you can keep playing on a new device (the developer can't see it; you can turn it off in Settings)
• A gentle break reminder appears after 30 minutes of continuous play (you can turn it off in Settings)
• Sound effects and vibration can each be turned on or off
• Works on iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo — including landscape, Split View and Stage Manager on iPad
• Available in 15 languages

■ Open Source
We publish our source code so anyone can check that this app shows no ads, has no purchases, and only connects to your own iCloud when iCloud sync is on.
```
